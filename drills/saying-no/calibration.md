# Calibration — Saying No

The gold-standard set the grader is anchored to: cases where the correct grade is known.
Three so far, all from one real exchange, so this is a start rather than a set with
coverage.

**This file is the method half**, as in `../dating-frame/calibration.md`, which owns the
reasoning for the split. The entries themselves — scenario, the practitioner's **verbatim**
replies, per-dimension reasoning, calibrated contrast — live in the private layer, in the
saying-no section of `<private area>/social-dynamics/calibration-entries.md`. What lands
here is the scenario *class*, the reply *class*, the correct grade and what the entry is in
the set to catch. **The reply text never lands, in any form** (`../context.md` step 5).

**How to use it.** Read it when `./context.md` is revised, and any time a grade feels off.
Run the private replies through the current grader and compare against the index. A
disagreement is resolved deliberately across both files, not absorbed into a session.

Dimensions are `./context.md`'s: **Detection** (D), **Specificity** (Sp), **Cost to the
relationship** (C), **Presence** (P).

## The index

| # | Scenario class | Reply class | D | Sp | C | P |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| N1 | A social invitation from someone with a continuing role in his life, repeated plainly | A playful line in place of an answer, then silence | **fail — missed** | **fail — hedged, at the limit** | pass, with a tax | **fail — cold withdrawal** |
| N2 | A same-day cancellation of a booked session with the same person | A clear no with one short state clause; no acknowledgement of her time, no next step | pass | pass | pass, with a tax | **fail — cold withdrawal, mild** |
| N3 | Plain care in reply to that cancellation, nothing crossed | A true warm sentence first, a short reason, unrefunded; present state left unanswered | pass | not applicable | pass | pass, with a tax |

**Which way a wrong grade errs.** N1 too kind, then too harsh. N2 too kind. N3 too harsh.

---

## What each entry is in the set to catch

- **N1** — silence is not a no. Skipping the thing is fine and is not the defect; the
  defect is that no no was sent. A grader reading "no pressure taken on" passes it. Small
  event, so a grader treating it as a breach errs the other way.
- **N2** — an apology is not always a refund. `../dating-frame/calibration.md` entry 10
  teaches that it is, and a grader carrying that across reads a missing sorry as security.
  Where someone's time was booked, a short sorry pays for something real.
- **N3** — the other half of this drill's bristling rule: when care is offered, the target
  is to take it. It fails towards too harsh: a grader keyed to neediness, or to the
  formality of the role, flags warmth that asks for nothing.

---

## The discrimination structure

**The relationship is an input to the grade.** This exchange was first graded without the
relationship stated, and read as dating. With the same words on the page, several grades
then changed. A grade here cannot be given until the grader knows what is owed between the
two people, and if that is missing the grader should ask, not assume.

**Gradient one — N1, N2, N3: how much of him arrives?** No message; a message with the no
and nothing else; a message with him in it. It runs across one exchange, which is why the
three are read together.

**Note what the set still lacks:** any clear crossing, guilt-trip, assumed favour or
re-litigated no. These three have no pressure in them at all, so the drill's core case is
still uncalibrated.
